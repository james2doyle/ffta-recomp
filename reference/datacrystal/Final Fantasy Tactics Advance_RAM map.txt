 Day/Month/Time 
 Month 
 The current Month is stored at 0x02002FBA in the RAM. 8-bit.
 Values:
 01 = Kingmoon 
 02 = Madmoon 
 03 = Sagemoon 
 04 = Huntmoon 
 05 = Bardmoon 
 Day/Date 
 The current Day (1-20) is stored at 0x02002FBB in the RAM. 8-bit.
 Play Time 
 The Hours are stored as a 16-bit value at 0x02002FBC.
The Minutes are stored as an 8-bit value at 0x02002FBE.
The Seconds are stored as an 8-bit value at 0x02002FBF.
 Mission Items 
 Mission Items are stored at 0x02002B08 to 0x02002C04 in a 4-byte data structure. There are 64 slots.
